2.5.0.112021 for Minecraft 1.12.2

All 33 gems come across from 1.11.2, along with the OreSpawn deposits, mining
XP, smith trades and both Fantasy Level settings. Use Java 8, Forge
14.23.5.2859 or later for 1.12.2, MMDLib 1.0.0 RC3 and MMD OreSpawn
4.1.6.112021. Base Metals is optional on this version.

- Pick up gems and their crafting materials to learn the recipes in the
  recipe book. It only teaches recipes allowed by your gem and Fantasy Level
  settings.
- Gem bars, plates, doors and shields have their own models. Ore overlays
  still use the stone texture from your resource pack.
- Keeps the Base Gems block and item IDs, config keys and Ore Dictionary
  names from 1.11.2.
- Kept tools, weapons and shields in High Fantasy and left player and horse
  armour to add-ons. Low Fantasy keeps gem processing, storage blocks and
  smith trades.

2.5.0.111021 for Minecraft 1.11.2

This port brings over the 33 gems, OreSpawn deposits, mining XP, smith trades
and both Fantasy Level settings from 1.10.2. Use Java 8, Forge 13.20.1.2588
or later for 1.11.2, Base Metals 2.5.1.111021 and MMD OreSpawn 4.1.6.111021.

- Updated smith trades and the Forge Mods config screen for Forge 1.11.2.
  You can still open the same config from OreSpawn.
- Updated the resource pack format so 1.11.2 loads all 18 language files.
  Gem ores still use overlays with the stone texture from your resource pack.
- Kept the block and item IDs, config keys and Ore Dictionary names from 1.10.2.
- Carried over ForgeGradle 7 and added a build check for the 1.11.2 branch.

2.5.0.110021 for Minecraft 1.10.2

Base Gems brings 33 gemstones to Minecraft 1.10.2. Use Java 8, Forge
12.18.3.2511 or later for 1.10.2, Base Metals 2.6.0.110021 and MMD OreSpawn
4.1.6.110021.

- Brought over the gem artwork and colours from 1.12.2. Ores use overlays
  so the stone from your resource pack shows through.
- Added Overworld deposits through OreSpawn. Common gems turn up in
  shallower caves, while ruby, sapphire and black diamond reward deeper
  mining. Existing chunks stay as they are.
- Gem ores give mining XP and support Fortune and Silk Touch. Smiths buy
  and sell gems as they level up.
- Added High and Low Fantasy and individual gem switches to the Forge and
  OreSpawn Mods menus. Restart after changing them. Low Fantasy keeps
  mining, trading and processing while removing recipes for gem equipment,
  gears and structural parts.
- Added Ore Dictionary names for gems and their material forms, including
  compound names such as gemBlackDiamond and gemBlueTopaz.
- Kept gem tools, weapons and shields in High Fantasy. Player and horse
  armour are left to add-ons.
- Translated gem names, blocks, equipment, fluids and config options across
  the same locale files as Base Metals.
- Updated the build and Eclipse setup to ForgeGradle 7. Minecraft still runs
  on Java 8.
